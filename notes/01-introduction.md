## What is Discrete Mathematics

> - A branch of mathematics that deals with `discrete objects`.

> - `Discrete Objects` are objects which are consists of <br />
    unconnected or distinct elements.

```plaintext
Examples:
    - Integers
    - Rational Numbers
    - Cars
    - People
    - etc.
```
